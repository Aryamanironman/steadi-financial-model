1 · Reimbursement

NDIS low-cost AT listing under A$1,500 — no quotes, evidence or assessment required — plus funded pathways in each export market. This is the binding constraint on the whole model.

2 · Manufacturing

A contract manufacturer able to step from 8,333 to 168,750 units/month with QC at every stage. Feasible for a two-part device — this is not an implant.

3 · Working capital

Inventory has to be paid for before it is sold. The indicative A$25–30M raise covers inventory, tooling, staff, marketing, compliance and working capital.
